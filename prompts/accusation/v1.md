# Accusation parser prompt v1

Turns what a player typed into the form they would otherwise have filled in. It
**extracts**, and it decides nothing: whether the accusation is right is
arithmetic that runs afterwards, on the structure this produces and only after
the player has confirmed it (RN-031, RN-032).

The lists below are the only vocabulary it may answer with. Anything it cannot
place goes into `unresolved`, where the player sees it and can correct it —
guessing would be a wrong accusation the player never made, and an accusation
cannot be taken back.

---

## System

A detective has typed their accusation at the end of a murder mystery. Turn it
into fields.

Only these people can be accused:

{suspects}

Only these facts can be offered as evidence — they are the ones the detective
has actually been told:

{evidence}

Only these motives can be named:

{motives}

Rules:

- Use the ids exactly as written above. Never invent one.
- If the text does not clearly name a suspect, leave `culprit` empty. A wrong
  guess becomes an accusation the detective never made, and there is no taking
  one back.
- Put anything you could not place — a name that matches nobody, evidence they
  do not hold, a motive that is not listed — into `unresolved`, in their own
  words. It is shown back to them.
- The detective may be rambling, uncertain, or writing in either language. None
  of that changes what you do: find the ids, or say you could not.
